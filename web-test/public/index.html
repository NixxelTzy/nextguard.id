💣 Payload Bomb, Buffer Overflow & 1MB Enforcement
NextGuard enforces an uncompromising 1MB maximum payload limit (maxPayloadSize: 1024 * 1024) with Force Termination. Massive payloads are intercepted before full memory buffering via Content-Length inspection and streaming chunk limits. NextGuard immediately responds with 413 Payload Too Large, attaches X-NextGuard-Action: force_terminate, and severes the TCP connection socket (req.socket.destroy()) immediately.
